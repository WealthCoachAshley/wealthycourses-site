How to Get an EIN for Free (2026 Step-by-Step Guide)

By Ashley Murray · Wealthy by Design · Over a decade in the financial industry

An EIN (Employer Identification Number) is your business's federal tax ID. You'll use it to open a business bank account, apply for licenses, and file business taxes.

It's free. Don't pay a website for it.

The IRS issues EINs at no cost on IRS.gov. If you apply online and your application is approved, your EIN is issued immediately. The IRS itself warns that some websites charge for this free service, so go only to IRS.gov.

What you need before you start

  • Your business entity type (sole proprietor, LLC, corporation and so on)
  • The Social Security number or ITIN of the "responsible party," the person who owns or controls the business
  • Your business's legal name and address

The steps

  1. Go to IRS.gov/EIN and choose to apply online.
  2. Answer the questions about your entity type and why you're applying.
  3. Enter the responsible party's information.
  4. Review and submit.
  5. Download and save your EIN confirmation notice. Your bank will ask for it.

Good to know

The IRS limits EINs to one per responsible party per day, and the online tool only runs during set hours. If you're forming an LLC, many people file the LLC first, then get the EIN under the LLC's name.

Small Business Tax Deductions: The Complete List (With % and Caps)

By Ashley Murray / Ramlochun · Serial Entrepreneur · Hawaii Broker · 10+ Years in Finance

Employees earn, get taxed, then spend what's left. Business owners earn, spend on legitimate business expenses, then get taxed on what's left. Same tax code, available to everyone — most people were just never shown how it works.

Every deduction below reduces taxable profit, not your tax bill dollar for dollar. What it's worth to you depends on your income, your tax bracket and your records, so this list shows the percentage deductible and the caps that matter, and your tax professional applies them to your situation.

The deduction list

Home officeBusiness-use % (or simplified)Regular method: the business-use percentage of your home (office sq ft ÷ home sq ft) applied to rent, or to mortgage interest, real estate taxes, utilities, insurance, repairs and depreciation, with no fixed dollar cap (the deduction can be limited if your business income is lower than your expenses). Simplified method: $5/sq ft up to 300 sq ft ($1,500 max). Either way, the space must be used regularly and exclusively for business. The rule that makes all of this real

A deduction you can't document is a deduction you lose. Three habits cover you:

  1. Separate accounts. Every business dollar flows through a business bank account and card. Your bank statement becomes a near-complete expense record.
  2. Capture receipts the day they happen. A photo in a dedicated folder is enough. Digital copies are accepted.
  3. Log the "why" for gray-area items. Meals and travel need the business purpose noted — one line is plenty.

Why this requires a real business

These deductions belong to businesses — not hobbies. That means registering a real entity, getting your free EIN from the IRS (ten minutes at irs.gov), and running the money separately. That structure is also what unlocks business credit under your EIN — the second half of the rulebook most people never learn.

FAQ

Do I need an LLC to take these deductions?

Business deductions attach to genuine business activity — sole proprietors can take them too. An entity like an LLC adds liability protection and structure, and makes the separation (banking, credit, records) much cleaner.

Can I deduct expenses from before my business officially started?

That's the startup-cost deduction: up to $5,000 of qualifying pre-launch costs in year one, with the remainder spread over 180 months (26 U.S.C. §195). Keep those early receipts.

What if I use something for both personal and business?

Deduct the business-use percentage — the honest split, applied consistently. The IRS says to separate the personal part from the business part; the personal part generally isn't deductible (IRS Pub 334). Phone, internet, vehicle, and home office all work this way.

When do quarterly estimated taxes start?

Sole proprietors and other individuals generally have to make estimated tax payments if they expect to owe $1,000 or more when they file (IRS: Estimated taxes). Estimated tax covers self-employment tax too, not just income tax. Set aside part of every payment you receive so the quarterly bill is never a surprise.

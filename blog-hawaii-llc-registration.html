How to Register an LLC in Hawaiʻi (Fees, Forms and Steps for 2026)

By Ashley Murray · Wealthy by Design · Over a decade in the financial industry

An LLC (limited liability company) is a business entity you register with the state. In Hawaiʻi, you file with the Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs (DCCA), Business Registration Division.

The form and the fee

You file Articles of Organization for a Limited Liability Company, Form LLC-1. The DCCA filing fee is $50, with an optional $25 for expedited service. You can file online through Hawaiʻi Business Express.

The steps

  1. Choose a name and check that it's available in the DCCA business search.
  2. Name a registered agent with a Hawaiʻi address. This can be you.
  3. File Form LLC-1 online and pay the fee.
  4. Get your EIN free from IRS.gov. Here's how.
  5. Get your General Excise Tax license from the Hawaiʻi Department of Taxation (Form BB-1, one-time $20 fee). Here's how.
  6. Open a business bank account.
  7. Mark your calendar for the annual report. The DCCA fee schedule lists it at $15.

An LLC doesn't replace good records or the right insurance, and the right structure depends on your situation. Talk to a CPA or attorney before you choose.

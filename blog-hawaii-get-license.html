How to Get a Hawaiʻi General Excise Tax (GET) License in 2026

By Ashley Murray · Wealthy by Design · Over a decade in the financial industry

Hawaiʻi doesn't have a traditional sales tax. Instead it has the General Excise Tax (GET), a tax on gross business income. According to the Hawaiʻi Department of Taxation, anyone who receives income from business activities in the state needs a GET license. That includes services, retail, rentals and more.

How to apply

  1. Go to Hawaiʻi Tax Online (hitax.hawaii.gov) and choose "Register New Business," or file Form BB-1 on paper.
  2. Enter your business details and your EIN or SSN.
  3. Pay the one-time $20 registration fee.

How long it takes

How you applyTime to get your Hawaiʻi Tax ID
OnlineAbout 5–7 days
Mail or drop-offAbout 4–6 weeks
In personImmediately

After you're licensed

GET returns are filed periodically, and your filing schedule depends on your business. Set calendar reminders the day you get your license. A tax professional can help you set up the right schedule.
